## Overview

Read Eazy — **Reazy** — is an Android application designed to provide reading and
learning support for students with dyslexia. It was developed under the *Going
Global Diversity and Inclusion Partnership* programme, funded by the British
Council in 2024, as a collaboration between Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM) and
Queen's University Belfast (QUB).

## Motivation

Dyslexia affects how people decode text, and conventional reading material and
apps rarely account for the typographic and pacing adjustments that make reading
more accessible. Reazy focuses on that gap for students, packaging accessibility
adjustments into a mobile experience that can be used both in the classroom and
at home.

## What it does

- Presents reading content with dyslexia-friendly typography and layout options.
- Provides reading-support features aimed at students working through text at
  their own pace.
- Runs on Android phones so it can be used outside of a dedicated computer lab.
