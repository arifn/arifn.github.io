## Overview

**IndoClimate** is a conversational AI system built to help disseminate climate
change information and policy updates within the Indonesian context. It is part
of the KONEKSI programme, a knowledge partnership funded by the Indonesian and
Australian governments (2023–2025), carried out with Deakin University.

## Motivation

Climate policy and scientific findings are often communicated in formats that are
hard for a general audience to navigate, and Indonesian-language material is
comparatively scarce. IndoClimate packages that information behind a chat
interface so that people can ask questions in natural language and get grounded,
context-appropriate answers.

## What it does

- Answers user questions about climate change and related policy in Indonesian.
- Grounds responses in curated source material to support the counter-infodemic
  goal of the wider project.
- Is deployed as a public tool rather than a research prototype only.
